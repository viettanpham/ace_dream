## Scope
This document defines approved and planned UI/UX directions for National Summary, Sector Summary, Base Details, Facility Drawers, and Historical Analytics.
Rule: Presentation may be inferred; gameplay balance may not.

## 1. National Summary Dashboard
- Macro presentation: Population, Regular Forces, Auxiliary Forces, Logistics Workforce, Economy/Treasury.
- Capacity bars with breakdown distributions.

## 2. Sector & Base Details
- Hierarchical UI: Header, Overview, Military, Logistics, Personnel, Economy, Security, Facilities.
- Direct status metrics: e.g. 920 HP/day repair with source breakdowns.

## 3. Facilities
- Detailed cards/drawers: SAR Centers, Rescue Docks, Repair Bays, Barracks, Academies.
