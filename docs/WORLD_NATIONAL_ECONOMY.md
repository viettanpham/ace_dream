## Scope
This document defines approved and planned architectural rules for National Treasury accounting, Gross Tax Revenue, Personnel Upkeep, Wartime Economic Degradation, Tax Cycles, and the National Transaction Ledger.

## 1. National Treasury Accounting
- Explicit accounting: Gross Revenue, Operating Expenses, Net Delta.
- Negative net balance is recorded as an Operating Deficit, never "negative tax".

## 2. Gross Tax Revenue & Modifiers
- Generated by citizens in sovereign VSS sectors (`sovereignty === 'vss'`).
- Modifiers: Base Per-Capita Rate, Stability Modifier, Distance Efficiency, War Disruption, Events.
- Uncontrolled sectors yield 0 tax revenue.

## 3. Personnel Upkeep
- Calculated strictly from ACTUAL living personnel counts, not maximum pool ceilings.
- Approved coefficients: Regular $\times 2.0$, Auxiliary $\times 1.0$, Logistics $\times 0.5$ Credits/person.

## 4. Tax Cycles & Transaction Ledger
- Default fiscal cycle: 1 in-game YEAR (configurable: DAY, MONTH, YEAR; testing: 2 DAYS).
- All financial events committed to National Ledger with unique transaction IDs.
