## Composition and pose rules
- Prefer **one character per image**, clean standalone artwork with background, no collage, UI, watermark, text or labels.
- Aim for high-resolution source (at least ~1122 × 1402 pixels where practical); final game assets are `.webp`.
- Diversify poses: seated side-view, standing three-quarter, walking, leaning, turning, working at a console, cockpit, interacting with medical gear, candid expressions; vary camera height, framing, lighting, hairstyles and colors. Avoid ten straight-on identical poses.
- Full-body frames must show feet and footwear; portrait/closeup may crop intentionally.
- Story illustration optional: **0–3 per chapter**, favor reuse of suitable Gallery art; avoid forcing every chapter to have new art.
